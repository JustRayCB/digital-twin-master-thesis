# MLG Tips

## Bad Examples

Plots: Title, legends, axis label, caption: What we need to understand/conclusion/highlight when seeing
Tune presentation so that it is understandable by a non-specialised audience in a field
Big font, not dark bg, contrasts

Show bullet point 1 by 1,  images 1 by 1 (animation/transition)
Beamer on 16:9, instead of 4:3

TOC useless if you do not specify clearly what you will explain

Avoid MATHS

Thank you/QUESTION slide -> TOC, overview of topics so that easier to catch questions

## Tips

Methodology (way to approach a problem)
Limitation (hypothesis, slightlly  work on specific state) include Future

10min presentation MAX 15
Video DEMO (+ comments LIVE)

Story with a continuity between the slides

Avoid going back and forth in the slides during the presentation

Do not explain every concepts in details. Do not over-do-it
Explain only what it is needed to understand the concepts of the thesis

Code/Docs/Demo/Tutorial -> 2/20

1 slide = 1 min

Send presentation before to Pascal/Bontempi to get a feedback
